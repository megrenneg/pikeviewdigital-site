Calvary Prayer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Calvary Prayer is a private prayer app for the congregation of Calvary Fellowship Fountain Valley (“the church,” “we,” “us”). The app is operated by the church, with Pikeview Digital as its developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

1. Who can use the app

The app is for members and attendees of the church. You create an account, and an administrator must approve it before you can see any prayer requests. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

3. Who can see your information

  • Approved members can see approved prayer requests and updates, the name shown on them, and how many people are praying. If you mark a request anonymous, the app does not display your name to other members.
  • Administrators can see the account list (name, email, phone, status), pending and rejected requests, and who submitted each request, including anonymous ones.
  • Technical note on anonymous requests. The name and account identifier of the person who submitted a request are stored with it so administrators can review it. The app does not show them for anonymous requests, but they are stored with the request’s data, which a technically skilled member could read outside the app. Do not rely on anonymous mode to hide your identity from members who may try to find it. We intend to improve this.
  • Service providers that run the app for us (section 4).
  • Legal requests. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Service providers

The app runs on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and App Check). Google processes your information on our behalf and under its own terms and privacy policy (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iPhone, the Apple Push Notification service. Device integrity checks use Google Play Integrity and Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck. Information is processed in the United States and possibly other countries where these providers operate.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account exists. Administrators may deactivate an account. A deactivated account is signed out and its data is kept until you ask for deletion or delete it yourself.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app: My Account → Delete Account, or Delete My Account on the “Account Pending Approval” screen. You will be asked for your password. Deletion cannot be undone. When you delete your account:

  • your profile (name, email, phone), notification settings and device tokens, “praying” marks, read and archive markers are deleted;
  • prayer requests of yours that were never approved (pending or rejected) are deleted;
  • prayer requests and updates that were already published to the congregation stay, but your name is replaced with “Former member” and your account identifier is removed from them.

Copies that exist in routine provider backups are removed on the provider’s normal schedule. If you cannot use the in-app option (for example, your account has been deactivated), email us at the address below and we will delete your data. If you want a published request of yours removed completely, ask an administrator or email us.

7. Security

Access is limited to approved accounts and enforced by server-side security rules, and data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share information in a request that you would not want the congregation to read.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change your name, phone number and notification settings in the app, turn notifications off in your device settings, and delete your account as described above. You may also email us to ask what information we hold about you or to ask us to correct or delete it. If you live in Colorado or another place with privacy laws, you may have additional rights, and we will honor valid requests.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell members in the app.
